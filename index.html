Backup & restore

Your records live only in this browser. Download a backup regularly and keep it with your farm files. A full backup is one JSON file with the farm file and attached photos. To move phone to PC: download (or share) on one device, restore on the other, and choose Merge — newest edits win, audit history is kept, photos fill in if the other device does not already have them. Older backups from before photos were packed still restore records; you will be told if photos are missing.

Import from a spreadsheet

Already keeping records in Excel or Google Sheets? Export them as CSV and bring your history in — match your columns once, and rows import as drafts you can finish and verify. Unknown products and fields are created automatically.

About

Pesticide Logger v2.8.1 — part of the Practical Farm Tools suite. A licensed, offline-first tool built for farms of every size; your farm records stay on this device.

This tool provides 50-state application recordkeeping field coverage with audit history, lot tracking, and in-cab workflows. It is not legal advice, does not replace WPS employer duties, and does not file California PUR, New York PRL, or other electronic reports for you. Always follow the product label and confirm requirements with your state pesticide agency.

Terms of use, license & privacy

Your data: all records live in this browser and in backup files you create. There is no account, server database, analytics, or telemetry. Optional EPA lookups and weather fetches send only the query — never your records. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it — use the backup tools.

Not legal advice: “Fields complete” means required fields are filled for your selected context — a checklist result, not a compliance determination or certification. State datasets marked partial/uncertain need agency confirmation; even researched entries can lag rule changes. The product label is the law.

License: a paid license is required after the 30-day trial to keep logging new applications. A key licenses one farm operation across its devices; keys verify offline and never phone home. Spray logs already on this device stay yours — review any year and export them even if a trial or subscription has ended. Refunds follow the payment processor’s policy.

No warranty: the software is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. To the maximum extent permitted by law, the authors are not liable for claims, fines, crop loss, or damages arising from its use, including reliance on compliance fields, deadlines, or interval calculations. Recordkeeping obligations remain yours.
