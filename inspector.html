What this is

This file opens without an account

The grower may hand you a tablet in inspector view, or an HTML packet. Both are the farm’s spray book for the visit. You do not need a login. You do not install software.

  1. Snapshot, not a lock. The packet is what was true when they saved it. The live log on their device can still be edited. History keeps the other version.
  2. Incomplete says incomplete. “Fields complete” means the boxes this state requires were filled. It is not a legal determination and not your finding.
  3. The label is the law. Rates, REI, and PHI on the packet were copied from the product label. This tool does not auto-fill those from EPA.
  4. Not an agency form. It is not a California PUR filing, a New York PRL filing, or a substitute for WPS employer duties.
  5. Check this file on a signed packet verifies the file was not changed after they saved it. It does not certify the spray was legal.

If they hand you the tablet: Exit leaves inspector view. An optional PIN, or the farm name, recovers a forgotten PIN. Ask them to print or save the packet if you need a copy that leaves with you.

Questions about a state’s field list belong with that state’s pesticide agency. Dataset holes (uncertain verification, unverified private duty) are named in the app — they are not hidden.
